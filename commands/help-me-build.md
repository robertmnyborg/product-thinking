# Help Me Build

Invoke the `product-thinking` skill and begin the interview.

Open with the dead-simple entry. **Never a template, never a blank page**:

> "Tell me in one sentence what you want to build."

If the user passed an idea as an argument, treat that as the one-sentence answer and go straight into the **Why Gate** (what / why / what's it for). Then follow the skill's arc: set the tier and the **build lane**. A self-serve automation (the author builds and operates it themselves: internal surface, token-shaped access, cheap failure) takes the **Self-Serve Build Route** and ends with the **Build Sheet**. Engineering-lane work runs Depth A (tool/spike) or Depth B (feature) and ends with the **Engineering Handoff Inputs** block, then **Phase 6**: say the relay out loud (spec → review → the engineer builds their plan from the brief, the spec, and any POC) and offer to write the spec now. Never stop at the brief.

Stay in the posture the skill defines: counter-argue, don't dictate; one question at a time; back off when the answer holds up. The person on the other side is not an engineer. Never ask them a "left or right" technical choice.
